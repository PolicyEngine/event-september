# Venue evaluation rubric for PolicyEngine event

## Evaluation criteria and scoring

Each venue is scored on a 1-5 scale across key criteria, with weighted importance for PolicyEngine's specific needs.

### Evaluation matrix

| Criteria | Weight | One Great George St - Brunel | BMA House | Myo New Street Square | Glaziers Hall | York Road |
|----------|--------|------------------------------|-----------|----------------------|---------------|-----------|
| **Cost effectiveness** | 25% | 5 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| **Location accessibility** | 20% | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| **Modern look and feel** | 15% | 3 | 2 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| **Capacity flexibility** | 15% | 5 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| **Professional atmosphere** | 10% | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| **AV/technical capabilities** | 10% | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| **Catering quality** | 5% | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| **Total weighted score** | 100% | **4.4** | **3.3** | **4.3** | **3.2** | **3.7** |

## Detailed scoring rationale

### 1. Cost effectiveness (25% weight)
- **5: Excellent** - Under £100pp all-inclusive
- **4: Good** - £100-120pp
- **3: Moderate** - £120-140pp  
- **2: Expensive** - £140-160pp
- **1: Very expensive** - Over £160pp

**Scores**: One Great George St (£94pp) = 5; BMA House (£130pp for 35) = 3; Myo (£50-120pp) = 4

### 2. Location accessibility (20% weight)
- **5: Excellent** - Westminster/Central, multiple transport links
- **4: Good** - Central London, near major stations
- **3: Moderate** - Good transport but less central
- **2: Fair** - Single transport option
- **1: Poor** - Difficult to reach

**Scores**: Westminster location scores highest (5), Bloomsbury/Holborn (4), Southbank/City (3)

### 3. Modern look and feel (15% weight)
- **5: Ultra-modern** - Contemporary design, cutting-edge facilities
- **4: Modern** - Updated, stylish interiors
- **3: Traditional modern** - Classic with modern touches
- **2: Traditional** - Historic/classic style
- **1: Dated** - Needs updating

**Scores**: Myo venues and York Road (5) for contemporary design; One Great George St (3) for traditional elegance; BMA House (2) for medical institution style

### 4. Capacity flexibility (15% weight)
- **5: Perfect fit** - No minimum, suits 35 exactly
- **4: Good flexibility** - Minimal overpayment
- **3: Some restrictions** - 10-20% overpayment
- **2: Poor fit** - Significant minimum issues
- **1: Unsuitable** - Major capacity mismatch

**Scores**: One Great George St (5) no minimum; Myo (4) 30 minimum; BMA House (2) requires 40 minimum

### 5. Professional atmosphere (10% weight)
- **5: Prestigious** - Regular government/policy venue
- **4: Professional** - Business-appropriate
- **3: Adequate** - Suitable for events
- **2: Casual** - Less formal
- **1: Inappropriate** - Not suitable for policy event

**Scores**: One Great George St and BMA House (5) for policy gravitas; Myo (4) for corporate professionalism

### 6. AV/technical capabilities (10% weight)
- **5: State-of-art** - Full tech suite, streaming capability
- **4: Comprehensive** - All standard AV included
- **3: Adequate** - Basic AV provided
- **2: Limited** - Some equipment available
- **1: Minimal** - Need to hire equipment

**Scores**: Myo and York Road (5) for modern tech; others (4) for solid provision

### 7. Catering quality (5% weight)
- **5: Premium** - Full service, high quality
- **4: Good** - Quality options included
- **3: Standard** - Basic refreshments
- **2: Limited** - Restrictions or separate
- **1: None** - Must arrange separately

**Scores**: BMA House (5) for comprehensive package; York Road (2) for venue restrictions

## Location analysis

### Westminster cluster (One Great George Street)
- **Advantages**: Heart of government, walkable to Parliament, prestige factor
- **Transport**: Westminster, St James's Park stations
- **Nearby**: Treasury, Cabinet Office, think tanks
- **Best for**: Maximising civil servant attendance

### Bloomsbury cluster (BMA House) 
- **Advantages**: Academic quarter, near universities
- **Transport**: Euston, King's Cross, Russell Square stations
- **Nearby**: UCL, LSE, British Library
- **Best for**: Academic and research audience

### City cluster (Myo Liverpool Street, Myo New Street Square)
- **Advantages**: Business district, modern facilities
- **Transport**: Liverpool Street, Farringdon, Chancery Lane
- **Nearby**: Financial institutions, law firms
- **Best for**: Private sector stakeholders

### South Bank (Myo Bankside, Glaziers Hall)
- **Advantages**: Cultural quarter, scenic views
- **Transport**: London Bridge, Southwark stations
- **Nearby**: Tate Modern, Borough Market
- **Best for**: Evening receptions, networking focus

## Final recommendation summary

**Primary choice: One Great George Street - Brunel Room**
- Highest overall score (4.4)
- Best combination of location, cost, and flexibility for 35 attendees
- Westminster location ideal for policy audience
- Traditional but professional atmosphere

**Alternative if modern aesthetic prioritised: Myo New Street Square**
- Score: 4.3 (very close second)
- Ultra-modern facilities and design
- Good City/Holborn location
- Requires 30 minimum (works for 35)
- Day package from £50pp

**If willing to pay for 40: BMA House**
- Despite lower flexibility score, offers premium experience
- Comprehensive package removes logistics concerns
- Strong reputation in policy circles
