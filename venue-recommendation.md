# Venue recommendation for PolicyEngine September 2025 event

## Executive summary

For an expected attendance of 35 people, I recommend **One Great George Street - Brunel Room** on **Friday 26th September 2025** as the primary choice for the PolicyEngine event. This venue offers flexibility without minimum attendance requirements and the date avoids Monday/Friday fatigue whilst providing good availability.

## Key requirements from event plan

- Central London location for accessibility
- Expected attendance: 35 people
- AV equipment for presentations
- Breakout spaces for networking
- Reliable wifi for demonstrations
- Professional setting suitable for policymakers and researchers

## Recommended venue: One Great George Street - Brunel Room

### Why One Great George Street

One Great George Street offers the best flexibility for 35 attendees without minimum number restrictions:

**Cost effectiveness**: £1,900 day rate plus approximately £40 per person for catering. For 35 attendees, the total cost would be approximately £3,300 (£94 per person).

**Prime Westminster location**: Located near Parliament, perfect for engaging policymakers and civil servants. Walking distance from Westminster and St James's Park stations.

**Professional atmosphere**: Historic venue with prestigious reputation, regularly used for policy events and conferences.

**Flexible space**: No minimum attendance requirements, making it cost-effective for 35 people whilst allowing room for growth if attendance increases.

**Facilities include**:
- Theatre-style seating for up to 54 guests
- Built-in AV equipment and screens
- Professional presentation facilities
- Networking space available
- Flexible catering options (coffee and sandwich lunch ~£40pp)

**Availability**: 26th September confirmed available

### Why Friday 26th September 2025

**Strategic timing**: End of September allows for summer holiday season to conclude and Parliament to be in session, maximising attendance from policymakers.

**Friday advantage**: Allows attendees to network more relaxedly without rushing back to offices, and out-of-London participants can travel Thursday evening if needed.

**Venue availability**: This date has the best venue availability across multiple options, providing flexibility if plans change.

### Alternative options considered

**BMA House - Prince's Room** (£95 + VAT/person): Excellent venue but requires minimum 40 attendees. Would need to pay for 40 people even with 35 attending, making it £4,560 + VAT (£130 per actual attendee).

**Myo venues** (Various prices): Modern spaces with good flexibility. Myo Liverpool Street or Myo New Street Square could work well with minimum 30 guests for packages at £60-120pp, but less traditional policy venue atmosphere.

**The Auditorium at York Road** (£950/hour + VAT): Could work for 3-hour event at £2,850 + VAT venue hire, but catering must be in separate space due to restrictions. Only 26th September available.

**Glaziers Hall - Bridge Room** (£2,750 + VAT room hire): Beautiful riverside location but expensive at approximately £4,770 total for 35 people (£136pp).
